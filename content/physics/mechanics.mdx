## Motion

Position $x(t)$ describes where an object is. Velocity and acceleration are derivatives:

$$
v = \frac{dx}{dt}, \qquad a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2x}{dt^2}.
$$

## Newton's second law

The net force on an object is related to its acceleration by

$$
\mathbf{F}_{net}=m\mathbf{a}.
$$

### Free-body diagrams

A free-body diagram isolates an object and shows the external forces acting on it. This turns a physical situation into equations that can be solved.

## Conservation of energy

Mechanical energy is often expressed as

$$
E = K + U,
$$

where $K$ is kinetic energy and $U$ is potential energy. In an isolated system, total energy is conserved.
