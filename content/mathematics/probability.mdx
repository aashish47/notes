## Events and probability

A probability assigns a number between $0$ and $1$ to an event.

$$
0 \le P(A) \le 1
$$

For mutually exclusive events $A$ and $B$,

$$
P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B).
$$

## Conditional probability

Conditional probability asks for the probability of $A$ after learning that $B$ occurred:

$$
P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.
$$

### Independence

Two events are independent when learning about one does not change the probability of the other:

$$
P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B).
$$

## Random variables

A random variable maps outcomes to numerical values. Common distributions include Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson, and normal distributions.

```text
Outcome -> random variable -> probability distribution
```
